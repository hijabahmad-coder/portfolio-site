About

Cybersecurity built the foundation. AI/ML is where I'm headed.

I'm a second-year BSCS student at PUCIT, University of the Punjab, Lahore, studying on merit-based scholarships. I started deep in cybersecurity — threat modelling, exploitation labs, log analysis — and I'm now pivoting toward AI/ML, keeping security as the niche that makes my work different from a typical ML portfolio.

Long term, I want to work at the intersection of AI and medicine — using reinforcement learning to help discover cures, in the spirit of Demis Hassabis's protein-folding work — or build something of my own in the AI space.

My path

How I got here

  1. Robotics, before code

    Two-year PGC Robotics certificate and 1st place at the UCP Robo Race — my first taste of building systems that actually have to work.

  2. Cybersecurity fundamentals

    Hands-on labs and coursework: OWASP Juice Shop vulnerability testing, STRIDE threat modelling, DFIR & SSH log analysis, DVWA labs, and buffer overflow exploitation.

  3. Discovering AI/ML

    Inspired by Demis Hassabis's work applying AI to real scientific problems, I built my own 26-week AI/ML roadmap — CS50P, daily DSA practice, and ML fundamentals — and started working it, day by day.

  4. Shipping projects

    My first standalone project (Library Book Tracker), an offline-first PWA (Ghar Ki Diary), and SecureHire — an AI interview coach for security roles — built for the AI Seekho 2026 Builders Day hackathon.

  5. What's ahead

    Contributing to open source through Hacktoberfest, working toward Google Summer of Code, and continuing to push AI/ML + security as my niche — with an eye on AI-for-medicine research down the line.
